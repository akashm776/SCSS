# Experiment C: construction × initial gradient-pressure calibration

Status: **protocol frozen; runner not implemented; GPU experiment not run**.
Experiment ID: `clip_pressure_v1`. This protocol was chosen after observing
[Experiment B](research-status-2026-10-07-difficulty.md). It is an exploratory
follow-up on reused seeds/reporting data, not an independent preregistration.

Question: **Does the later semi-hard synthetic advantage survive calibration of
both constructions to the same initial mean auxiliary/native gradient-norm targets?**

The machine-readable [protocol](../configs/clip_pressure.json), deterministic
[calibration plan](../configs/clip_pressure_calibration_plan.json), and this document
are pinned by [the freeze manifest](../configs/clip_pressure_freeze_manifest.json).
Protocol changes require a new version and a recorded reason before new outcomes.

## Frozen calibration procedure

Select 256 images per seed from B's training population, excluding the union of
both continuation streams and the historical frozen probes. Reporting images and
old excluded images are already outside the training population. Rank eligible
indices by SHA256 of UTF-8 `scss-pressure-calibration-v1:{seed}:{source_index}`,
breaking a hash tie by source index; take the first 256 and partition into four
ordered batches of 64. The pinned eligible counts are **278, 286, 296** in seed
order. Calibration images may have appeared in the preceding native history.

Use the same four batches and update-100 caption epoch for both weight checkpoints,
constructions, targets and continuation streams within a seed. Use B's training
forward/precision and all trainable parameters. Native and auxiliary gradients on
a batch must share the same forward/RNG realization. Preserve model weights,
buffers, modes, gradients and all optimizer/scheduler/RNG state around calibration.
No optimizer update or reporting data participates.

The auxiliary gradient is explicitly **unweighted**, evaluated at alpha = 1:

```text
g_N[b] = grad_theta L_native[b]
g_a[b] = grad_theta L_aux,a[b] at alpha = 1
r[b] = ||g_a[b]||_2 / ||g_N[b]||_2
c[s,w,a] = mean_b r[b]                      # four batches
alpha[s,w,a,rho] = rho / c[s,w,a]
```

This is a mean of per-batch norm ratios, not the norm of an averaged gradient or a
ratio of mean norms. Save all four individual native/auxiliary norms and ratios,
their mean, each coefficient, calibration identities and state-preservation audit.
Coefficients are generated by the frozen procedure without reporting outcomes;
the treatment is `(construction, target rho, calibration procedure)`.

Recheck actual gradients of `alpha * L_aux` on those same calibration batches,
saving each weighted norm and ratio. Require the mean ratio to be within **5%
relative error** of the target. Require each native and unweighted auxiliary norm
to be finite and greater than **1e-12** and each coefficient to be finite/positive.
Failure stops calibration; no silent coefficient clipping, new target, fallback
batch, iterative outcome tuning or automatic retry. Preserve failures in the record.

Persist and verify calibration records for both weights before launching any
continuation branches for that seed. Each coefficient remains fixed for all 50
updates and is shared across its two continuation streams. No online normalization.

## Analysis and interpretation hierarchy

Use arm-minus-native loss `D_H(w,a,rho)`, where negative helps. Define:

```text
Q_H(w,rho) = D_H(w,semi,rho) - D_H(w,hard,rho)
```

Average streams within seed, then seeds equally. The single primary is:

```text
M_late = [Q_50(500,0.02) + Q_50(500,0.07)] / 2
```

Freeze the following interpretation order; the secondary contrasts are not
additional primary tests:

1. **Later relative advantage:** primary `M_late`; report every seed and stream.
2. **Agreement across strengths:** show both late Q values and prespecified
   construction × pressure interaction `G_rho = Q_50(500,0.07) - Q_50(500,0.02)`.
   An average must not hide opposite rankings.
3. **Dependence on weight state:** report both early Q values, pressure-specific
   age interactions, and `I = mean_rho[Q_50(500,rho) - Q_50(100,rho)]`.
   A directional crossover at a target requires early Q > 0 and later Q < 0.
4. **Absolute utility, separately:** show every arm versus native at each weight
   and target. Relative success is Q < 0; absolute success is D < 0. A less harmful
   intervention can still be worse than abstaining.

Primary horizon **50**; report **0, 1, 5, 10, 25, 50**. Earlier horizons and
bidirectional reporting-pool R@1 changes in percentage points are secondary.
No favorable horizon selection, trimming, fitted controller, significance or
equivalence claim. Three reused training seeds are the replication units.

## Pressure and construction diagnostics

Before updates at offsets **0, 10, 25, 49**, record native and weighted auxiliary
norms, ratio, cosine, total pre-clipping norm and clipping activation. At each
update retain B's support overlap/separation and actual contributor/generated
margin audits. These are observational; they never change a coefficient or action.

The target controls an initial mean calibration-batch norm ratio. It does not match
each batch, parameter, parameter group, AdamW-preconditioned update, clipping
behavior or future pressure. Show individual ratios and observed pressure drift;
do not claim perfect trajectory pressure matching or isolated representation causation.

If a relative advantage persists, the direct conclusion is that it persists after
this specified initial calibration. If the gap shrinks, that is consistent with
pressure explaining part of B, not proof that pressure explains all of it. Opposite
rankings at the two strengths support a construction × strength interaction in
the tested range. Neither finding is a retrieval win or a universal curriculum law.
